How I Think

The Principles Behind Every Build.

01
Problems First, Tools Second

I don't pick a stack and look for problems to fit it. I understand what the system needs to do, then choose the right tools. That's why the SBA project uses Scikit-Learn, not a trendy LLM wrapper.

02
Full Ownership

From data acquisition to final report, from wireframe to deployment. I don't hand off the hard parts. Seven years of freelance work trained me to own every layer of a project.

03
Clean Code Over Clever Code

A system that a new team member can read in an afternoon is worth more than one that impresses no one except the person who wrote it. Readability is a feature.

04
International Perspective

Having studied and worked across India, the USA, and Canada, I understand that good systems have to work for people with different contexts, tools, and expectations.
