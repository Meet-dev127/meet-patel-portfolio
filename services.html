Good to Know

Questions before we start.

How long does a typical project take?+

It depends on scope. A focused dashboard or automation can land in one to two weeks once scoped; a full web platform typically runs three to eight weeks. You get a fixed timeline in the proposal before any work begins.

How do you price work?+

Most projects are quoted as a fixed price against a defined scope, so there are no surprises. Ongoing work is a monthly capacity arrangement. Share your goal and budget range in the contact form and I will tell you honestly what fits.

Do you work remotely and with clients outside Canada?+

Yes. Freelance and contract work is fully remote, and I have worked across time zones since 2018. For full-time employment I am focused on Canadian-based roles (remote, hybrid, or in-person in New Brunswick).

I am not sure exactly what I need. Can you still help?+

Absolutely. Describe the problem rather than the solution and we will scope it together on a short call. Often the right fix spans a couple of these disciplines, which is exactly why having one owner across web, data, and AI helps.
